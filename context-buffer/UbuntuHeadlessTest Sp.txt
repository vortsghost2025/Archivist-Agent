## Important scoring rule

Use this vocabulary:

```text
PASS = works end-to-end automatically
PARTIAL = detects/reports but does not remediate, or requires manual step
FAIL = missing, broken, or unsafe
BLOCKED = correctly blocked by governance
UNKNOWN = insufficient evidence
```

Do not label something `PASS` just because one subcomponent works.

Example:

```text
Contradiction detection: PASS
Contradiction adjudication: PARTIAL or FAIL
Automatic graph mutation: BLOCKED
```
